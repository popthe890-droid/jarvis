What can I say?
  • "hello", "what time is it", "what's the date"
  • "tell me a joke", "flip a coin", "roll a dice"
  • "5 plus 7" (also minus, times, divided by)
  • "open YouTube" (Google, Gmail, Maps, Spotify, Netflix, GitHub)
  • "search for cool robots", "play lofi music"
  • "what's the weather"
  • "open school Gmail", "open personal Drive" (after you add accounts below)
  • "start a 25 minute focus timer" (focus sessions get an automatic 5 minute break)
  • "set a timer for 10 minutes", "how much time is left", "cancel the timer"
  • "good morning" (your briefing: date, weather, to-do list, fun fact)
  • "add buy a handle to my list", "what's on my list", "remove handle from my list"
  • "hollow", "MC" (opens your games; add more in "My apps and games")
  • "tell me a fun fact", "roast me"
  • "make the orb red" (blue, green, purple, gold)
  • "butler mode", "sarcastic mode", "hype mode", "normal mode"

      My apps and games

      Say a word and it opens that app. Your browser will ask "Open this app?" the first time. Tick "Always allow" so it stops asking. Steam games use steam://rungameid/ plus the game's number (it's in the game's Steam store web address). Other links that work: ms-settings:, calculator:, spotify:, discord:// or any website.

        My Google accounts

        Add a nickname and the email for each account. Then say "open school Gmail" or "open personal Drive". Works with Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, YouTube, Classroom and Meet. Saved in this browser only.
